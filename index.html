TERMS & CONDITIONS

Please review these terms before confirming your booking. They apply to custom package requests, photography sessions, videography coverage, event films, and related creative services provided by Tony Photography.

Booking Confirmation

Your booking is confirmed only after date availability is approved and the agreed advance payment is received. Until then, the requested slot remains open.

Pricing & Custom Quotes

Package pricing depends on event type, hours of coverage, travel, crew size, edit scope, and add-ons. Final pricing is confirmed after discussion and approval.

Travel & Extra Charges

Travel, accommodation, special permissions, venue restrictions, and extended shoot hours may involve additional charges whenever applicable.

Deliverables

Deliverables may include photos, reels, teaser videos, films, albums, and drone coverage based on the selected package. Raw files are not included unless agreed in writing.

Delivery Timeline

Delivery time depends on event scale and post-production requirements. Priority or express delivery must be requested before booking and may include extra cost.

Cancellation & Rescheduling

Cancellations or date changes should be shared as early as possible. Advance payments may be non-refundable, and rescheduling depends on availability.

Client Responsibilities

The client must provide correct event details, timing, location access, permissions, and important moments to capture. Missing information can affect coverage quality.

Communication Consent

By submitting the package form, you agree to be contacted by WhatsApp or phone regarding your inquiry, quotation, and booking follow-up.

If you need custom commercial rights, confidential event coverage, or special contract terms, these can be discussed before final booking confirmation.
