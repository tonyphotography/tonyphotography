Got Questions?

FREQUENTLY ASKED

How far in advance should I book? +
We recommend booking at least 2–4 weeks in advance for events, and 2–3 months ahead for weddings, especially during peak season (October–March). Popular dates fill up fast!
Do you travel outside Hyderabad? +
Yes! We cover events pan-India and have shot in 50+ cities. Travel beyond 50 km from Hyderabad incurs travel & accommodation costs, discussed and agreed upon upfront.
How long until I receive my photos? +
Standard delivery is 7–15 business days for photos, and 21–30 days for wedding films. Our Next-Day Delivery add-on delivers a social-ready gallery within 24 hours of your event.
Can I request a specific editing style? +
Absolutely. During consultation, share references or a mood board and Tony will custom-tailor the colour grade and edit to match your aesthetic. Up to 3 revision rounds are included.
What is your payment process? +
A 30% advance is required to confirm your booking. The remaining balance is due 7 days before your event. We accept UPI, bank transfer, and cash. All payments are confirmed via WhatsApp.
What if something goes wrong on the day? +
We carry professional backup equipment for every shoot — cameras, lenses, batteries, and storage. In the rare case of data loss, please refer to our Terms & Conditions for our liability policy.
